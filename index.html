M'AKOMA is a living bracelet for your inner circle.

M'AKOMA is my newly launched startup: a jewelry-first presence wearable where a small number of people you love can live as physical beads. It is built around chosen presence, not feeds, follower counts, public likes, or algorithmic noise.

The first product is designed for short, intimate signals: a pulse, a glow, or a voice charm from someone who matters. The goal is simple: make closeness feel physical again without turning friendship into another screen.
